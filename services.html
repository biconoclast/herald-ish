So you have a story, but don’t know what (if anything) about it isn’t working. Agents don’t request and betas either ghost you or give you confusing feedback. All you want is some undivided attention.

I am here to help.

As a developmental editor, I specialize in Science Fiction and Fantasy novels and graphic novels. We’ll work on your world-building: your environments, your industries, your governments, your cultures, your technologies and hard sciences, or magics if that’s your flavor. I have a degree in linguistics, so bring on your constructed languages.

However, novels are stories, not encyclopedias. I want your main character’s arc to hit, and I will be looking at your tension and resonance to make that happen. You can count on me to fill in your plot holes and tighten up your throughline. And I won’t let you get away with a soggy middle, a lackluster reveal, or a let-down ending.

Whether you’re trying to break into traditional publishing or looking to self-publish, I’m here to help you turn your story into the most robust version of itself that will resonate with your audience.

Ten-Page Trial

I have been developing my philosophies about storytelling for more than two decades. So have other editors, many of whom will have come to different conclusions than I have. For an author, it’s critical to feel that your vision is understood and supported by your editor. You should come away from an edit letter feeling empowered to improve your story, not misunderstood and more lost than when you started. For this reason, I recommend starting with a $30 ten-page (2,500 words) trial edit. You will receive in-line notes and suggestions as well as a short edit letter explaining what I believe is and is not working in your opening pages. If this gives you the confidence to invest in a full evaluation or developmental edit afterwards, the cost of the trial will be deducted from that service fee.

Manuscript Evaluation

So you have a finished book, and you’ve edited it to the best of your ability. You’ve read the books and articles, watched the talking heads, but you have your doubts. Maybe the feedback has been mixed or the engagement has been low or you’ve had no luck in the query trenches or are worried that will happen if you try. For $0.01 per word, I will read the manuscript cover to cover, so to speak, and you will receive an edit letter explaining what I believe is and isn’t working with the manuscript, as well as actionable feedback and suggestions for revision. I don’t follow a feedback formula or give a grade on story beats or anything; the letter will be tailored to the work itself. This service includes a follow-up coaching call, so you can ask questions and run any brain-storming ideas by me (I am liable to brainstorm as well). If you prefer, this can be a series of email exchanges instead of a call.

Developmental Edit

So you have a book, perhaps finished, perhaps not, and you just really want full hands-on support for it. Much more than an edit letter alone will give. With this service at $0.03 per word, in addition to a personalized comprehensive edit letter, I will leave in-line comments in the manuscript document itself. These will address any issues I see, be them relating to character voice and believability, pacing, structure, payoffs, other story elements, as well as sentence-level clarity. This is NOT a copyedit, that is between you and your manual of style, but I will flag grammatical issues too, and may rewrite sections to demonstrate better clarity, readability, or pacing. (Also, if I leave comments yelling at your characters, this is a good sign.) I do accept incomplete manuscripts for this service, but they must be at least 50,000 words long and have explanations or outlines of the missing sections. If you’re stuck with an even less complete manuscript, consider my Book Coaching service instead. This service includes a follow-up coaching call, so you can ask questions and run any brain-storming ideas by me (I am liable to brainstorm as well). If you prefer, this can be a series of email exchanges instead of a call.

Redlining

If you are both the writer and illustrator on a project, or are a team submitting together, this can be an add-on to your graphic novel edit. For $2 per page, I will go over your drawn pages–preferably your roughs or thumbnails, but hey, illustrators have to revise too–in classic red. Mostly I will be looking to improve your clarity and readability (no, you may not squeeze that speech bubble into the margin like an afterthought), but as always, this will be tailored to the individual project. If I see a recurring issue, I will provide thoughts on how to correct it.
