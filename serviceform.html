AI Policy

As there are many kinds of artificial intelligence developed to perform different tasks, I do think it is appropriate to specify that this policy concerns generative AI. When I say genAI, I mean image generators and large language models that generate images and text on demand.

My promise to you is that I do not use them. These systems were already trained on my stolen labor, and I refuse to train them even more for free. Everything I write to you, I myself wrote. I will never share any property you entrust to me with any third party, especially not with a genAI which will only steal your work for its training data.

I will also not ask you to disclose any use of genAI. I will certainly never run the text through an AI “detector” as first, that is sharing with a third party and second, that goes into its training data and becomes theft. I will not accuse you of using an LLM, even if I suspect you have.

I will simply give you my notes on it, which will be no use to you if you did not write it.
I will give you my thoughts, which will be of no help if you did not think.
I will make arguments, which you will have no defense against if you made no decisions.
As for art, what good are my red-lines to you if you do not draw? Stable diffusion is not drawing. It won’t understand my advice for correction.
